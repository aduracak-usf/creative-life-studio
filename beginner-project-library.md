# Beginner Creative Project Library

Time estimates are approximate active work time. Drying, pressing, and setup may add elapsed time. Split a project into short sessions when needed.

## Fabric bookmark
- Category: Sewing
- Skill: Beginner
- Time: 30–45 active minutes
- Materials: two felt rectangles, needle, embroidery thread, scissors
- Steps: cut matching shapes; stack and secure; stitch around the edge with a running stitch; tie off and trim.
- First action: choose two felt colors.

## Scrunchie
- Category: Sewing
- Skill: Beginner with careful machine use
- Time: 45–75 active minutes
- Materials: woven cotton strip, elastic, thread, safety pin or bodkin, sewing machine or hand needle
- Steps: sew the fabric strip into a tube; turn it right-side out; thread elastic through; secure elastic ends; close the opening.
- First action: measure a cotton scrap and check the project pattern's dimensions.
- Caution: use the machine manual, keep hands clear of the needle, and turn the machine off before adjustments.

## Quilted coaster
- Category: Sewing
- Skill: Beginner
- Time: 45–75 active minutes
- Materials: two compatible cotton squares, thin batting, thread, pins or clips
- Steps: stack the layers; stitch a simple grid; trim evenly; finish edges with binding or a suitable turned-edge method.
- First action: choose washable cotton scraps and compatible batting.

## Simple pouch
- Category: Sewing
- Skill: Beginner to intermediate
- Time: 60–120 active minutes depending on closure and lining
- Materials: stable woven fabric, thread, scissors, clips, and a closure if desired
- Steps: choose a basic pouch pattern; cut stable pieces; sew seams following the pattern; finish raw edges; turn and press safely.
- First action: decide what the pouch should hold and find a beginner pattern with matching fabric requirements.

## Mini floral painting
- Category: Painting
- Skill: Beginner
- Time: 20–40 active minutes plus drying
- Materials: thick paper, two or three colors, brush, water if needed, scrap paper
- Steps: test a limited palette; place a few loose stems; build petals with repeated brush marks; add small leaves; let it dry.
- First action: pick a flower shape and two colors.

## Abstract color study
- Category: Painting
- Skill: Beginner
- Time: 15–30 active minutes plus drying
- Materials: paper, two colors, brush or colored pencils
- Steps: make three swatches; choose a repeated shape; vary size and spacing; add one contrasting accent.
- First action: choose a two-color pairing from objects nearby.

## Upcycled paper collage
- Category: DIY
- Skill: Beginner
- Time: 15–30 active minutes
- Materials: scrap paper, scissors, glue stick, backing paper
- Steps: choose a simple theme; tear or cut shapes; arrange before gluing; attach from the background forward.
- First action: gather three paper scraps in related colors.
