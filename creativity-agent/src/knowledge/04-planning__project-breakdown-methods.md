# Project Breakdown Methods

**Collection:** Planning  
**Audience:** Creative Life Studio visitors and The Creative Companion  
**Purpose:** Transform inspiration into realistic milestones and actionable tasks.

## Standard stages

1. Define outcome and constraints. 2. Confirm materials and tools. 3. Prepare/cut/sketch. 4. Construct or paint. 5. Finish and inspect. 6. Document or celebrate completion.

## Task design

Each task should have a verb, tangible result, approximate duration, and dependencies. Example: "Cut two 5-inch cotton squares (10 min)" is better than "work on coaster." Measurements depend on actual project instructions.

## Quilted coaster example

**Session A, 15 min:** choose fabrics, measure/cut layers. **Session B, 15–25 min:** pin and sew perimeter. **Session C, 15–35 min:** turn, press, close gap, topstitch. Confirm construction method and safety with hot iron.

## Progress tracking

Use statuses: Someday, Planned, In Progress, Paused, Completed. Record next action and last completed task. Percent progress may be tasks completed / total tasks, but tasks can differ in effort.

## Handling interruptions

Before stopping, record where materials are stored, what was completed, any problem encountered, and the next action. A good restart note prevents repeated setup.

## How the Creative Companion should use this resource

Give practical, situation-specific advice. Ask about time, experience, supplies, and energy only when needed. Distinguish estimates from guarantees. Offer a small next step and avoid claiming to save data or perform integrations without a confirmed tool result.
