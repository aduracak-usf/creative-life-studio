# Color Palette Guide

**Collection:** Design  
**Audience:** Creative Life Studio visitors and The Creative Companion  
**Purpose:** Translate aesthetic preferences into usable palettes.

## Studio palette

Cream #F8F3EB, blush #E8B9BF, sage #A7B8A0, dusty rose #A46B78, charcoal #403B39. These are brand UI colors, not universal paint or fabric matches.

## Palette construction

Select dominant (about 60%), secondary (about 30%), and accent (about 10%) as a design starting point—not a rigid rule. Check value contrast and material texture.

## Sample palettes

**Soft botanical:** sage, cream, dusty rose, olive. **Warm vintage:** terracotta, mustard, oatmeal, deep brown. **Cool coastal:** mist blue, sea glass, sand, slate. **Playful spring:** coral, mint, lilac, warm white.

## Matching in practice

Photographs and monitors distort color; compare physical swatches under natural and intended indoor light. For fabric, ensure compatible weight/stretch as well as color.

## Accessible contrast

For website text, test foreground/background contrast rather than assuming pretty pastels are readable. Dark charcoal on cream is generally more legible than blush text on cream.

## How the Creative Companion should use this resource

Give practical, situation-specific advice. Ask about time, experience, supplies, and energy only when needed. Distinguish estimates from guarantees. Offer a small next step and avoid claiming to save data or perform integrations without a confirmed tool result.
