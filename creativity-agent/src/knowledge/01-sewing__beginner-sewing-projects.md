# Beginner Sewing Project Library

**Collection:** Sewing  
**Audience:** Creative Life Studio visitors and The Creative Companion  
**Purpose:** Provide concrete beginner projects with realistic session plans.

## Fabric bookmark

**Time:** ~20–40 min. **Materials:** two cotton rectangles, optional light interfacing, thread, ruler. **Steps:** cut matching pieces; place right sides together; stitch edges leaving turning gap; clip corners carefully; turn and press; topstitch to close. **15-min split:** choose/cut; stitch/turn; press/topstitch.

## Simple scrunchie

**Time:** ~30–60 min. **Materials:** strip of lightweight fabric, elastic, safety pin or bodkin, thread. **Steps:** sew long fabric tube; turn; thread elastic; securely join elastic ends; close fabric opening. Adjust dimensions to desired size and available pattern; do not prescribe one-size-fits-all dimensions.

## Quilted coaster

**Time:** ~45–75 min. **Materials:** two cotton squares, batting, thread, ruler, pins/clips. **Steps:** cut layers; arrange right sides and batting according to chosen construction method; stitch perimeter leaving gap; trim corners; turn and press; close and topstitch/quilt. **Sessions:** 15 min prep, 15–25 min assembly, 15–35 min finishing.

## Envelope pillow cover

**Time:** ~60–120 min. **Materials:** woven fabric, pillow insert, thread. **Steps:** measure insert and allow for seams/overlap; hem back-panel edges; overlap back panels on front right sides together; stitch perimeter; finish seams; turn and press.

## Simple tote bag

**Time:** ~90–180 min for beginners. **Materials:** sturdy cotton/canvas, straps, thread, optional interfacing. **Steps:** measure/cut panels; make straps; stitch side/bottom seams; finish raw edges; attach straps securely; reinforce stress points.

## Selection rules

For 5–15 minutes, suggest a phase of a project, not a full tote or quilt. For limited supplies, choose a bookmark or hand-sewn felt ornament. For beginners, start with straight seams and stable cotton.

## How the Creative Companion should use this resource

Give practical, situation-specific advice. Ask about time, experience, supplies, and energy only when needed. Distinguish estimates from guarantees. Offer a small next step and avoid claiming to save data or perform integrations without a confirmed tool result.
