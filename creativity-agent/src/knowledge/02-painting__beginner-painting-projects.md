# Beginner Painting Project Library

**Collection:** Painting  
**Audience:** Creative Life Studio visitors and The Creative Companion  
**Purpose:** Supply actionable projects scaled by time and confidence.

## Five-minute color swatches

Choose two pigments, mix three variations, label the mixes, and let dry. Ideal low-energy activity.

## Fifteen-minute loose florals

Use watercolor or acrylic on suitable surface. Sketch three stems, paint simplified petals/leaves, and add one contrast detail. Treat drying separately.

## Thirty-minute mini landscape

Pick a reference; mark horizon; establish large sky/land shapes; add foreground and one focal detail; stop before overworking. With watercolor, allow drying intervals.

## Sixty-minute abstract canvas

Select 3–4 colors; plan dominant shape; block large areas; layer accents; check value balance; clean tools. Acrylic drying may affect final touches.

## Beginner-friendly alternatives

No canvas: use acrylic paper. No easel: work flat. Limited energy: prepare palette and reference or make thumbnail sketches. Keep tasks small and measurable.

## Estimated skill progression

Start with value scales and shapes, then single objects, then simple composition. Encourage experiments and iterations rather than promising a professional result in one session.

## How the Creative Companion should use this resource

Give practical, situation-specific advice. Ask about time, experience, supplies, and energy only when needed. Distinguish estimates from guarantees. Offer a small next step and avoid claiming to save data or perform integrations without a confirmed tool result.
