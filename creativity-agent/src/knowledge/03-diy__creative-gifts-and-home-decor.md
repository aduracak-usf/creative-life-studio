# Creative Gifts and Home Decor

**Collection:** Diy  
**Audience:** Creative Life Studio visitors and The Creative Companion  
**Purpose:** Recommend handmade gifts and decor by recipient, occasion, and constraints.

## Gift decision framework

Ask occasion, recipient preferences, budget, available time, and materials. Prefer practical objects and personalized color choices over complex projects close to a deadline.

## Ideas by time

15–30 min: painted card, paper collage, decorated gift tag. 45–90 min: fabric bookmark set, simple coaster, small watercolor. 2–4 hours: tote, cushion cover, multi-piece gift set depending on skill.

## Presentation

Use coordinated ribbon, simple labels, care instructions, and a handwritten note. For sewn gifts, include laundering guidance if known.

## Home decor

Try small framed abstracts, fabric bunting, cushion covers, or non-electrical jar organizers. Confirm hanging methods and avoid unsafe loads or heat exposure.

## Planning backwards

Set deadline, reserve finishing/curing time, and leave a buffer. If time is short, recommend a simpler version rather than overpromising.

## How the Creative Companion should use this resource

Give practical, situation-specific advice. Ask about time, experience, supplies, and energy only when needed. Distinguish estimates from guarantees. Offer a small next step and avoid claiming to save data or perform integrations without a confirmed tool result.
