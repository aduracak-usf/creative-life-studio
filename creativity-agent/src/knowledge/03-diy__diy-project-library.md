# DIY Project Library

**Collection:** Diy  
**Audience:** Creative Life Studio visitors and The Creative Companion  
**Purpose:** Provide accessible craft options with realistic materials and cautions.

## Paper collage greeting card

**Time:** 15–30 min. **Materials:** cardstock, scrap paper, glue stick, scissors. **Steps:** choose palette; cut 3–5 shapes; arrange without glue; attach; add message. Low-cost and beginner-friendly.

## Fabric scrap bunting

**Time:** 45–90 min. **Materials:** scraps, string/bias tape, scissors, needle/thread or fabric glue suited to use. **Steps:** make triangle template; cut pieces; arrange; attach to tape; hang away from heat sources.

## Decorated storage jar

**Time:** 30–60 min plus curing. **Materials:** clean jar, suitable paint/adhesive, labels, decorative paper. **Steps:** clean/dry; plan pattern; decorate exterior; cure as label directs. Do not imply decorative finishes are food-safe.

## Pressed-flower bookmark

**Time:** 20–40 min active plus pressing/drying time. **Materials:** dried pressed flowers, paper, adhesive, protective sleeve/laminate if available. **Steps:** arrange; secure; protect; trim.

## Selection

Match project to existing materials, mess tolerance, and drying time. For children or shared spaces, choose age-appropriate tools and supervision.

## How the Creative Companion should use this resource

Give practical, situation-specific advice. Ask about time, experience, supplies, and energy only when needed. Distinguish estimates from guarantees. Offer a small next step and avoid claiming to save data or perform integrations without a confirmed tool result.
