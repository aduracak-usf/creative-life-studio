# Materials and Supply Selection

**Collection:** Design  
**Audience:** Creative Life Studio visitors and The Creative Companion  
**Purpose:** Recommend supplies appropriate to project, skill, budget, and safety.

## Start with inventory

Ask what is already owned. Separate essential materials from optional upgrades. Prefer affordable, multipurpose tools for beginners.

## Sewing essentials

Stable cotton, matching thread, needles suitable for fabric, pins/clips, fabric scissors, ruler, seam ripper, iron where safe, machine or hand-sewing kit. Rotary cutter and mat are optional; observe blade safety.

## Painting essentials

Appropriate surface, beginner pigment set, a few brushes, palette, water containers or cleaning materials, cloth. Choose watercolor paper for watercolor and primed surfaces for acrylic.

## DIY essentials

Cardstock, scissors, glue suitable for material, measuring tools, safe storage. For adhesives and finishes, follow labels, curing time, ventilation and surface compatibility.

## Substitution rubric

Compare function, strength, washability, weight, flexibility, heat tolerance, and finish. Avoid recommending an adhesive for structural load or an unsuitable paint for food-contact surfaces.

## Budget tiers

No-buy: use scraps and existing tools. Low budget: purchase only missing essentials. Investment: upgrade after repeated use demonstrates need.

## How the Creative Companion should use this resource

Give practical, situation-specific advice. Ask about time, experience, supplies, and energy only when needed. Distinguish estimates from guarantees. Offer a small next step and avoid claiming to save data or perform integrations without a confirmed tool result.
