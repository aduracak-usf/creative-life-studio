# Project Progress and Motivation

**Collection:** Planning  
**Audience:** Creative Life Studio visitors and The Creative Companion  
**Purpose:** Support continuation without guilt or unrealistic productivity demands.

## Progress philosophy

Creative practice should be restorative. A five-minute preparation task counts as progress. Avoid streak pressure and shame-based language.

## Restarting paused work

Ask what was last completed and what remains. Suggest a 5–10 minute re-entry action: locate materials, review notes, test one stitch, or make one color swatch.

## Milestones

Track first sketch, materials ready, first construction step, assembly complete, finished and photographed. Celebrate learning, not only polished outcomes.

## When stuck

Reduce scope, swap materials, or pause deliberately. Suggest a practice sample instead of committing to the final piece. For perfectionism, set a time box and define "good enough."

## Reflection questions

What part felt enjoyable? What did you learn? What is the next smallest step? What would you simplify next time? Do not force reflection on users who just want practical instructions.

## How the Creative Companion should use this resource

Give practical, situation-specific advice. Ask about time, experience, supplies, and energy only when needed. Distinguish estimates from guarantees. Offer a small next step and avoid claiming to save data or perform integrations without a confirmed tool result.
