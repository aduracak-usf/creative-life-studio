# Sewing Fundamentals

**Collection:** Sewing  
**Audience:** Creative Life Studio visitors and The Creative Companion  
**Purpose:** Teach foundational sewing concepts and safe, reliable techniques.

## Essential vocabulary

**Seam allowance:** distance between fabric edge and stitching line; many patterns use 1/4 in (6 mm) for quilting or 5/8 in (1.5 cm) for garments, but always follow the pattern. **Right sides together:** printed/finished faces touch before sewing. **Backstitch:** a few reverse stitches to secure a seam. **Press:** lift and set an iron rather than dragging and distorting the fabric. **Grainline:** direction parallel to the selvage; affects stretch and drape.

## A dependable first seam

1. Prewash fabric when the project requires it and press wrinkles out. 2. Cut accurately using a ruler and fabric-safe scissors or rotary cutter. 3. Pin or clip with edges aligned. 4. Test stitch length and tension on scraps. 5. Sew slowly with consistent seam allowance; keep fingers clear of the needle. 6. Press seam as directed; trim or finish raw edges if needed.

## Hand-sewing basics

Use a running stitch for basting, backstitch for stronger seams, and a slip/ladder stitch for closing small openings invisibly. Choose a needle and thread appropriate for fabric weight; knot and secure thread ends.

## Beginner mistakes

Puckering may come from unsuitable needle, tension, or pulling fabric. Uneven seams often improve with a seam guide. Fraying calls for zigzag, overlock, pinking, or enclosed seam finishes. Always test on scraps first.

## Safety

Switch off the machine before changing needles or cleaning. Keep hands away from the presser-foot needle area; use clips instead of sewing over pins. Handle rotary cutters and hot irons carefully and unplug irons when finished.

## How the Creative Companion should use this resource

Give practical, situation-specific advice. Ask about time, experience, supplies, and energy only when needed. Distinguish estimates from guarantees. Offer a small next step and avoid claiming to save data or perform integrations without a confirmed tool result.
