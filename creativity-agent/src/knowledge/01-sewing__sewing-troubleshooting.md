# Sewing Troubleshooting

**Collection:** Sewing  
**Audience:** Creative Life Studio visitors and The Creative Companion  
**Purpose:** Diagnose common beginner sewing problems using reversible, low-risk checks.

## Triage checklist

Ask what machine and fabric are used, what changed, and whether issue occurs on scraps. Stop sewing if needle bends, hits plate, or machine jams. Recheck correct needle, threading, bobbin, presser foot, and fabric compatibility.

## Uneven seams

Mark seam allowance with tape or seam guide; reduce speed; guide fabric lightly instead of pulling. Practice consistent start/stop and corner pivots.

## Puckering

Try fresh appropriate needle, adjust stitch length, check tension on scrap, and avoid pulling. Stabilizer may help lightweight or slippery fabrics. Press gently rather than stretching seams.

## Skipped stitches

Replace needle, use correct point for woven versus knit, ensure full insertion and correct orientation, and rethread. Persistent skipping may need service.

## Wavy knit seams

Use stretch stitch or narrow zigzag, ballpoint/stretch needle, and avoid stretching fabric while sewing. Walking foot or stabilizer can help.

## Poor pressing or crooked cutting

Press before cutting, square fabric carefully, and measure twice. A slightly imperfect first project is still usable; suggest the simplest repair before restarting.

## How the Creative Companion should use this resource

Give practical, situation-specific advice. Ask about time, experience, supplies, and energy only when needed. Distinguish estimates from guarantees. Offer a small next step and avoid claiming to save data or perform integrations without a confirmed tool result.
