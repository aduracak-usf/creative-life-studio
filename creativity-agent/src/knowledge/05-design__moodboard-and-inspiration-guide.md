# Moodboards and Inspiration Boards

**Collection:** Design  
**Audience:** Creative Life Studio visitors and The Creative Companion  
**Purpose:** Help users turn collected images into a specific creative direction.

## Collect intentionally

Choose 3–8 references representing color, texture, shape, and intended function. Record why each reference matters rather than collecting endlessly.

## Extract a project brief

From the board identify: mood (cozy, modern, playful), palette, key materials, scale, technique, constraints, and one first project.

## Example

Board: sage florals, cream cotton, vintage stitching, small kitchen decor. Project: pair of quilted coasters; palette sage/cream/rose; technique straight seams and topstitch; next step measure fabric scraps.

## Image handling

Store only images users have permission to use; distinguish inspiration from copying another maker’s design. Credit sources where appropriate. For localStorage websites, large images can exhaust browser quota; resize/compress and warn about local-only storage.

## Avoid inspiration overload

After collecting a few references, prompt for a decision: choose one palette, one material set, and one 15-minute next action.

## How the Creative Companion should use this resource

Give practical, situation-specific advice. Ask about time, experience, supplies, and energy only when needed. Distinguish estimates from guarantees. Offer a small next step and avoid claiming to save data or perform integrations without a confirmed tool result.
