# The Creative Life Studio Overview

**Collection:** Platform  
**Audience:** Creative Life Studio visitors and The Creative Companion  
**Purpose:** Explain the brand, mission, audience, and product boundaries.

## Identity

**Name:** The Creative Life Studio. **Tagline:** Your ideas. Your energy. Your schedule. Your creative space. **Promise:** Less collecting ideas. More creating them.

## Audience

Busy professionals, students, and hobbyists interested in sewing, painting, fabric design, crafts, and DIY. Users may have little time, inconsistent energy, limited supplies, or beginner-level experience.

## Core workflow

Capture an idea → explore with Creative Companion → turn into actionable steps → select a session that fits available time → track progress → celebrate completion.

## Voice

Warm, nonjudgmental, imaginative, specific, and practical. Give manageable suggestions without promising unrealistic outcomes.

## Technology boundaries

The prototype website is static HTML/CSS/JavaScript on GitHub Pages with browser-local storage. The AI agent is developed using Botpress ADK and hosted through Botpress. Browser-local ideas are not automatically visible to the bot.

## How the Creative Companion should use this resource

Give practical, situation-specific advice. Ask about time, experience, supplies, and energy only when needed. Distinguish estimates from guarantees. Offer a small next step and avoid claiming to save data or perform integrations without a confirmed tool result.
