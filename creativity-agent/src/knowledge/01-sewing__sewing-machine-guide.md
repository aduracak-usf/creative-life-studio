# Sewing Machine Guide

**Collection:** Sewing  
**Audience:** Creative Life Studio visitors and The Creative Companion  
**Purpose:** Explain setup, maintenance, and first-line diagnosis without assuming a specific machine model.

## Before starting

Check the machine manual for threading path, bobbin orientation, needle insertion, and compatible feet. Fit a fresh needle appropriate for the fabric; use quality all-purpose thread for basic woven cotton. Thread the upper path with presser foot raised if the machine requires it, then lower before sewing.

## First test

On two scraps of the actual project fabric, sew a straight seam. Check both sides for balanced stitches. Use a medium stitch length (often around 2.5 mm for standard woven seams); adjust to material and machine guidance.

## Common symptoms and likely checks

**Loops underneath:** rethread upper thread, confirm tension disks are engaged, and check presser foot position. **Skipped stitches:** replace bent/dull needle, check needle type and insertion. **Thread breaking:** inspect threading, burrs, needle eye and tension. **Fabric not feeding:** check feed dogs, presser foot, stitch length; do not force fabric. **Bird nesting at start:** hold thread tails for initial stitches and verify threading.

## Maintenance

Unplug before cleaning. Remove lint around bobbin area per manual; do not blow lint deeper into machine. Oil only where manufacturer specifies. If a needle strikes metal, the machine jams repeatedly, or timing seems off, seek qualified service.

## Teaching sequence

Practice on paper without thread only if the manual permits, then sew straight lines on scraps, pivot corners with needle down, and finally complete a simple bookmark or napkin.

## How the Creative Companion should use this resource

Give practical, situation-specific advice. Ask about time, experience, supplies, and energy only when needed. Distinguish estimates from guarantees. Offer a small next step and avoid claiming to save data or perform integrations without a confirmed tool result.
