# Time and Energy Creative Planning

**Collection:** Planning  
**Audience:** Creative Life Studio visitors and The Creative Companion  
**Purpose:** Make suggestions realistic for limited time and variable energy.

## Time tiers

**5 min:** choose reference, gather supplies, label a palette, sketch thumbnail. **15 min:** cut pieces, paint swatches, stitch one seam, prepare a project kit. **30 min:** complete a small phase or a simple card. **60 min:** focused project session with setup and cleanup time.

## Energy tiers

**Low:** low-decision, low-setup activities like organizing materials or choosing colors. **Medium:** measured cutting, sketching, short practice. **High:** machine sewing, multi-step assembly, painting with multiple layers when enough time exists.

## The next-small-step method

Define a task that can be started in under two minutes: "put fabric and ruler on the table" rather than "finish quilt." Then offer an optional stretch goal.

## Estimates and buffers

Include setup, cleanup, mistakes, and drying/curing time. If a project is 90 minutes total, offer 3–5 sessions rather than claiming it fits one 15-minute window.

## Personalization example

User: "I have 15 minutes, low energy, and floral cotton." Response: "Choose two coordinating solids and cut a paper coaster template today; save machine sewing for a higher-energy session."

## How the Creative Companion should use this resource

Give practical, situation-specific advice. Ask about time, experience, supplies, and energy only when needed. Distinguish estimates from guarantees. Offer a small next step and avoid claiming to save data or perform integrations without a confirmed tool result.
