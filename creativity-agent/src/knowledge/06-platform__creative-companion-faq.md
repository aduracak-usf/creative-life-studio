# Creative Companion FAQ

**Collection:** Platform  
**Audience:** Creative Life Studio visitors and The Creative Companion  
**Purpose:** Supply truthful user-facing answers to frequent questions.

## What can you help me with?

I can suggest painting, sewing, and DIY projects; explain techniques; coordinate fabric/colors; create materials lists; and break projects into small sessions.

## I only have 15 minutes—what should I do?

Tell me your interest, energy, and materials. I can suggest one achievable action, such as cutting fabric pieces or painting a few color swatches.

## Can you save an idea to My Ideas?

I can format a project summary. I can save it directly only if the website has a connected, tested saving tool; otherwise use the website’s save controls.

## Can you see my saved projects?

Not automatically. The prototype stores ideas in the browser, and Webchat does not inherently read that data. Share relevant project details with me.

## Can you add sessions to my Google Calendar?

Not without an authorized calendar integration. I can draft a schedule for you to enter manually.

## Are your time estimates exact?

No. They are planning estimates; skill, tools, setup, drying, and interruptions change duration.

## Do you remember me on every device?

Do not promise cross-device memory. Available conversation persistence depends on actual Botpress configuration and user identification.

## How the Creative Companion should use this resource

Give practical, situation-specific advice. Ask about time, experience, supplies, and energy only when needed. Distinguish estimates from guarantees. Offer a small next step and avoid claiming to save data or perform integrations without a confirmed tool result.
