# Watercolor Techniques

**Collection:** Painting  
**Audience:** Creative Life Studio visitors and The Creative Companion  
**Purpose:** Teach water control and approachable watercolor exercises.

## Materials and preparation

Watercolor paper around 140 lb/300 gsm is a common beginner choice; tape edges if useful. Prepare two water jars if possible, absorbent cloth, and round brush.

## Core techniques

**Wet-on-dry:** pigment onto dry paper for sharper edges. **Wet-on-wet:** pigment onto damp paper for blooms and soft transitions. **Glazing:** transparent layer over fully dry paint. **Lifting:** remove pigment with clean damp brush/tissue while conditions permit. **Dry brush:** textured strokes with limited water.

## 15-minute mini floral

2 min choose two colors; 3 min practice petals on scrap; 7 min paint loose flowers and stems; 3 min evaluate and leave to dry. Drying time may exceed active painting time.

## Troubleshooting

Muddy color: limit repeated scrubbing, use fewer pigments, let layers dry. Cauliflowers: uneven drying and extra water; use deliberately or control moisture. Buckling: heavier paper and less water.

## Beginner projects

Botanical greeting card (30–60 min active), simple sky gradient (15–30 min), leaf study (15–30 min), limited-palette landscape (45–90 min).

## How the Creative Companion should use this resource

Give practical, situation-specific advice. Ask about time, experience, supplies, and energy only when needed. Distinguish estimates from guarantees. Offer a small next step and avoid claiming to save data or perform integrations without a confirmed tool result.
