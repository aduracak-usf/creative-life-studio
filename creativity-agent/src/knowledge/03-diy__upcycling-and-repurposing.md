# Upcycling and Repurposing

**Collection:** Diy  
**Audience:** Creative Life Studio visitors and The Creative Companion  
**Purpose:** Turn existing materials into creative projects responsibly.

## Inventory first

List usable fabric remnants, old shirts, containers, paper, buttons, ribbons, and packaging. Inspect cleanliness, damage, and suitability before recommending reuse.

## Project matches

Cotton shirt → reusable cleaning cloth or simple drawstring pouch (if fabric suitable). Denim scraps → sturdy patchwork coaster or pocket organizer. Cardboard → templates or collage backing. Glass jar → non-food decorative storage.

## Practical constraints

Avoid repurposing contaminated containers for food or cosmetics. Watch sharp edges on cans and glass. Check flammability and heat resistance before suggesting candles or lamps; prefer non-electrical decor for beginners.

## Design coherence

Pick a unifying color palette or material texture so reused pieces feel intentional. Use visible mending as a design feature where appropriate.

## Small sessions

5 min sort scraps; 15 min plan and cut; 30 min assemble; 60 min finish a small project. Distinguish hands-on time from washing, curing, or drying.

## How the Creative Companion should use this resource

Give practical, situation-specific advice. Ask about time, experience, supplies, and energy only when needed. Distinguish estimates from guarantees. Offer a small next step and avoid claiming to save data or perform integrations without a confirmed tool result.
