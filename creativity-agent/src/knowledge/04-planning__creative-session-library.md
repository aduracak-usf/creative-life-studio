# Creative Session Library

**Collection:** Planning  
**Audience:** Creative Life Studio visitors and The Creative Companion  
**Purpose:** Offer a catalog of creative activities by duration and energy.

## Five-minute sessions

Painting: make two color swatches; choose a reference; sketch a thumbnail. Sewing: wind bobbin; select fabric pairing; label pattern pieces. DIY: sort buttons; choose collage palette; clear work surface.

## Fifteen-minute sessions

Painting: loose floral study; practice five brushstrokes; block one acrylic background. Sewing: cut bookmark pieces; sew one straight seam; press fabric and prep template. DIY: make gift tags; arrange paper collage; prepare a jar label.

## Thirty-minute sessions

Painting: small watercolor gradient card; limited-palette landscape sketch. Sewing: assemble simple bookmark if pre-cut; prepare scrunchie tube. DIY: finish collage card; decorate storage container exterior.

## Sixty-minute sessions

Painting: mini acrylic abstract with drying allowance; botanical watercolor composition. Sewing: beginner coaster assembly or simple scrunchie with setup. DIY: fabric bunting segment or a set of handmade cards.

## Selection algorithm

First match safety/available tools and skill, then time and energy, then interests. Offer one primary recommendation and up to two alternatives; explicitly separate active work from drying or curing.

## How the Creative Companion should use this resource

Give practical, situation-specific advice. Ask about time, experience, supplies, and energy only when needed. Distinguish estimates from guarantees. Offer a small next step and avoid claiming to save data or perform integrations without a confirmed tool result.
